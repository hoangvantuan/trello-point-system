Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

What is Point System?

Point System is a Trello Power-Up that lets teams log and track effort points on Trello cards.

Data we collect

Point System does not collect, transmit, or store any data on external servers. All data is stored exclusively in Trello's own pluginData storage, which lives on Trello's infrastructure and is governed by Atlassian's Privacy Policy.

Specifically, the Power-Up stores:

  • Point entries (value, date, comment) per card
  • Estimate targets per card
  • Member ID, username, and display name (provided by Trello) to attribute entries

Data we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No cookies
  • No personal data beyond what Trello provides
  • No data sent to third-party services

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties. Period.

Data retention

Data persists in Trello's pluginData as long as the card exists. Removing the Power-Up from a board deletes all associated pluginData.
